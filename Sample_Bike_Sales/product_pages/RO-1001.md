## Care & Maintenance Tips

To keep your bike performing at its best, follow these simple guidelines:

1. **Clean regularly** — Wipe down the frame after wet rides and wash the drivetrain with a gentle degreaser every few weeks.
2. **Check tire pressure** — Maintain the recommended PSI printed on the tire sidewall for optimal rolling resistance and comfort.
3. **Lubricate the chain** — Apply chain lube every 200–300 km or after riding in rain to prevent rust and ensure smooth shifting.
4. **Inspect brakes** — Verify brake pad wear and cable tension before every ride for safe, responsive stopping.
5. **Schedule a tune-up** — Bring your bike to a certified service center at least once a year for a full inspection.
